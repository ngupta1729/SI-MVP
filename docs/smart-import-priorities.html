Hypothesis and Priorities for Smart Import

Operating hypothesis

Smart Import usage is mostly enterprise — and enterprise doesn’t churn because of Smart Import, so tuning it for paid-user retention moves a number that won’t move. The lever is trial activation — converting 14-day trial users to paid.

A 14-day trial converts on conviction: “this did my real job, at a quality I trust, and I could live in it.” That comes from completing the loop once, for real — own source in, generate, refine what’s off, approve, walk away with something they’d put in front of learners.

We are optimising upstream first.
