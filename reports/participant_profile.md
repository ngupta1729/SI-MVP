## Project Idea

A reworked **H5P Smart Import** — the AI feature in H5P.com that turns a source document into H5P
interactive content. The project redesigns that workflow end-to-end: how educators author intent,
review and approve what the AI generates, refine and remix it, and find it afterward. Delivered
as a working prototype (reproduces Smart Import's generate-from-source behaviour, renders in the
real H5P player, layers the redesigned workflow on top) plus a product/UX spec. The early product
walkthrough was only to learn the existing flow — the project is dedicated to Smart Import, not a
general "product twin" tool.

### Problem space

Smart Import's week-1 → week-2 retention is ~20%: educators try it once and don't come back. The
first AI output isn't trusted, or costs too much to clean up. Target users: educators and
instructional designers who create H5P interactive content.

### Success criteria

A working prototype of the reworked Smart Import workflow, demoed as if live on a source chosen
in the moment, that a mentor would trust enough to put in front of learners with only light
review. _(Tentative — to be refined in the Ideation workout.)_

### Starting autonomy level

**L2 — AI as Collaborator.** The AI drafts the spec and prototype changes; the PM reviews,
adjusts, and approves each pass. This is a **starting point, not a commitment** — the expectation
is to level up toward L3 (AI as Agent) as trust in what the AI can be relied on for grows.
